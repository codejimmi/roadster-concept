Active Aerodynamics

Adaptive rear wing and multi-mode suction system. High-downforce or low-drag modes selectable. Track Mode lowers the car and transforms suspension behavior.

  • Zero-speed downforce
  • Optimus-actuated wing
  • Steer-by-wire system

Cold-Gas Thrusters

Compressed-air thrusters inspired by SpaceX technology. Rechargeable onboard. Deliver thrust beyond tire traction limits for acceleration, stability, and (on Rocket) brief flight capability.

  • ~600 L air capacity (Rocket)
  • Micro-second adjustments
  • Self-recharging tanks

Powertrain

Rear-wheel-drive dual-motor layout. Each motor has its own inverter enabling precise torque vectoring. High-power battery pack optimized for sustained output and fast charging.

  • 800–1,000 V architecture
  • ~1 MW peak output
  • Rapid Supercharging

Three Versions. One Vision.

From road-legal performance to limited-run race machines and an invite-only air-capable variant.
